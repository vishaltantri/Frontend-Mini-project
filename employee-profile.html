Employee profile

A staff profile from NovaTech’s design team, using headings and inline formatting tags to give a summary, a rating and two formulas the right emphasis.

Ananya Rao

Senior Product Designer

NovaTech Systems · Bengaluru

Performance rating 4.8 / 5 · Outstanding

Professional summary

Ananya leads the design systems group at NovaTech Systems, where she turns research findings into components that teams can actually ship. She has eight years of experience across fintech and education products, and her work is known for being accessible by default rather than by retrofit.

She is currently mentoring four junior designers and running a monthly critique series. Her favourite compliment from a colleague: “She makes the complicated feel obvious.”
