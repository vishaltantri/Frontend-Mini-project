Exercise 25 Drop-down Multi-select

Course registration

Choose a department and one or more programming languages. The name is checked with a regular expression, then the complete selection is shown as a summary.

Semester elective registration

Open to second-year CSE students. Each student may choose up to four languages.

Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on macOS) to select more than one language.

Limited seats: CSE 60, IT 45, ECE 30, EEE 30. Allocation is confirmed by the academic office.

What this page demonstrates

  • Student name text box
  • Department drop-down (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE)
  • Multiple-selection programming-language list box
  • Name validated with an alphabets-and-spaces regex
  • Department and language selection required
  • Entered details displayed after validation
