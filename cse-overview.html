A department built around strong fundamentals, applied research and a habit of shipping things that work.

Computer science, in context

The Department of Computer Science at VIT Vellore brings together algorithms, systems, data and human-centred computing. Undergraduate students begin with programming and discrete mathematics, then branch into electives such as distributed systems, computer vision, information security and interaction design.

Teaching is project-led: every semester includes a studio or lab course where a team takes an idea from requirement to demo. The department also runs weekly seminars, an annual technical symposium and a peer-mentoring programme for first-year students.
