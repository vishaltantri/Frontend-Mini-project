NovaTech Systems

Design-led engineering for learning

NovaTech Systems builds software for schools, colleges and the people who run them. Our mission is to make everyday administrative work disappear into the background, so teachers and students can spend their attention on learning. We design a connected campus platform that covers admissions, attendance, assessments and communication. Every product starts with field research and a prototype tested with real users. Today, more than four hundred institutions across India run on NovaTech, and our team of sixty works from Bengaluru and Coimbatore.

About the Company

Founded in 2016 by three engineers and a teacher, NovaTech began as a timetable tool for a single school. The company is bootstrapped, profitable and deliberately small. We work in six-week product cycles, publish our accessibility checklist openly and give every employee one day a month for community teaching.

Products & Services

Campus OS — admissions, attendance, fees and parent communication.

Classroom Kit — lesson planning, question banks and low-stakes quizzes.

Nova Insights — dashboards that turn term data into decisions.

Implementation studio — migration, training and a named success partner.
