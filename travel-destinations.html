Travel Destinations

Four destination cards for the Frontend Lab travel desk — one outer container, four sections, each with an image, a heading and a short description.

East Asia

Japan

Kyoto’s lanes, Tokyo’s neon and a rail pass that makes both feel close. Go in spring for the blossom or in November for quiet temples and red maple.

Cherry blossomsRail travelStreet food
Central Europe

Switzerland

Glacier trains, lakeside villages and trails that start at the railway station. Lauterbrunnen is the base camp; the Bernina Express is the long way home.

AlpsScenic railHiking
North America

Canada

Banff’s turquoise lakes, Vancouver’s coast and long summer days in British Columbia. Rent a car, wake early, and let the mountains set the pace.

RockiesRoad tripNational parks
South Asia

India

Start with the Taj Mahal at sunrise, then take the slow route south — Hampi’s boulders, Kerala’s backwaters and the long train between them.

HeritageBackwatersRail

What this page demonstrates

  • One main div holding all destination cards
  • Four separate destination div sections
  • An image, heading and two–three line description each
  • Vertical stacking without relying on a grid framework
